Most real problems don't sit with one profession.

About us

People you can trust

Two partners, with decades of combined experience in planning, execution and managing complex projects. They know the market's biggest gap up close — that the customer is forced to be their own contractor, and to hope no one takes advantage of them. The network, knowledge and connections they've built over the years go into every project, so you get the best result — without running between ten different parties, and without worrying who to trust.

Ofer Maman

Ofer brings decades of experience managing construction, renovation and building-maintenance projects. He's fluent in reading plans, preparing bills of quantities and managing contractors and subcontractors — with a background as a property assessor. Among other roles he managed building-restoration projects at CleanCor, worked alongside architects and insurance adjusters, and ran construction projects worth tens of millions of shekels. That hands-on know-how is what lets Benefit not just plan — but execute in the field, down to the last detail.

Oded Dorzbacher

Decades of experience managing industrial companies and complex projects — from manufacturing and robotics to logistics. Oded has led multidisciplinary companies and projects end to end, specializing in work-process optimization through LEAN, Kaizen and Six Sigma. That experience is exactly what lets Benefit orchestrate many professionals into a single solution — and take responsibility for execution in the field.
