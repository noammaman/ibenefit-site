Restoration expertise

Restoring from the ground up — by type of damage

Restoring from the foundation and bringing the building back to itself, after fire, water and natural damage — alongside general renovation. We don't just clean; we manage the entire restoration, every professional in it, until we hand over a finished building.

Fire & smoke damage

A fire leaves far more than burns — soot, odor and damaged structure. We restore until no trace remains.

  • Soot cleaning from walls & surfaces
  • Burnt-odor neutralization
  • Structural & finish restoration

Water & flood damage

A leak, burst pipe or flood — every hour counts. We dry, treat the source, and prevent recurring damage.

  • Extraction & drying, incl. sub-floor
  • Locating & treating the source
  • Restoring floors & walls

Nature & weather damage

A storm, rain or flood that left the building damaged — we seal, repair and bring it back to strength.

  • Wind, rain & flood damage
  • Sealing & repair
  • Exterior & interior restoration

Damp & mold

Stains, peeling and a musty smell signal a problem beneath the surface. We treat the root, not just the symptom.

  • Locating the source of damp
  • Mold removal
  • Sealing & root treatment

Odor & return to normal

After any damage, the small details are what bring back the feeling of home. We close every corner, to handover.

  • Odor neutralization
  • Thorough cleaning
  • Contents care

General renovation

You don't always need an event. A neglected or aging building deserves to work again — and we orchestrate the whole renovation.

  • Interior & exterior renovation
  • Systems upgrade
  • Orchestrating every professional
How the process works

Five steps — from damage to handover

You do one thing: pick up the phone. We manage everything else.

1

Arrival & assessment

We come to the site, assess the extent of the damage, and explain what needs to happen — in plain language.

2

Plan & budget

We build an orderly restoration plan with clear timelines and cost, before anything begins.

3

Assemble the team

We recruit and coordinate every professional needed — you talk to us, not to them.

4

Execute & supervise

We carry out the restoration under close supervision, and keep you updated along the way.

5

Handover

We hand over a finished, clean, working building — as if nothing had happened.

And with the insurance company: We're experienced in working with insurers and loss adjusters, and guide you through that side too — so you can focus on getting back to normal, not on dealing with the parties.
